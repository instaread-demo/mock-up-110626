5 Female Country Superstars Who Haven’t Won CMA Entertainer of the Year – 2 Have Never Been Nominated

The CMA Entertainer of the Year award is the biggest individual honor handed out by the Country Music Association — and for female artists, simply earning a nomination can be a major career milestone.

Only 10 women have won the award in CMA history, with Lainey Wilson becoming the most recent addition to that group.

She won in 2023 and 2025, joining a short list that includes Loretta Lynn, Dolly Parton, Reba McEntire, Shania Twain and Taylor Swift.

The 2026 CMA Awards will take place Nov. 18 at Nashville's Bridgestone Arena, with Wilson serving as host.

Read More: Do Country Artists Owe It to Fans to Attend Awards Shows?

While women are once again well represented among this year's nominees, some of country music's biggest female stars have never taken home the night's top prize — and two have never even been nominated for it.

Miranda Lambert

Miranda Lambert has been nominated for CMA Entertainer of the Year six times but has never won the category.

That's despite an impressive overall CMA résumé. Lambert has earned 70 nominations and 14 wins, making her one of the most decorated women in CMA history.

CMA Awards: 70 nominations, 14 wins
Entertainer of the Year: 6 nominations, 0 wins

Carrie Underwood

Carrie Underwood has also received six Entertainer of the Year nominations without winning the category.

The American Idol winner has collected seven CMA Awards from 40 nominations overall.

CMA Awards: 40 nominations, 7 wins
Entertainer of the Year: 6 nominations, 0 wins

Faith Hill

Faith Hill has received one Entertainer of the Year nomination but has never won the award.

Overall, Hill has earned 22 CMA nominations and three wins.

CMA Awards: 22 nominations, 3 wins
Entertainer of the Year: 1 nomination, 0 wins

Martina McBride

Martina McBride is one of the most surprising names on this list because she never received an Entertainer of the Year nomination during her career.

She earned 30 CMA nominations and five wins, but the top category has eluded her entirely.

CMA Awards: 30 nominations, 5 wins
Entertainer of the Year: 0 nominations

Kacey Musgraves

Kacey Musgraves has also never been nominated for CMA Entertainer of the Year.

The seven-time CMA winner has received 27 nominations overall, making her absence from the top category particularly notable.

CMA Awards: 27 nominations, 7 wins
Entertainer of the Year: 0 nominations

2026 CMA Awards Nominations - Snubs + Surprises Reveal a Dangerous Trend

This isn't the year for fans of traditional country music to complain. Anyone who does it like Alan, and George was rewarded while those who pushed the boundaries of the genre were forgotten. Here are seven snubs and two surprises from the 2026 CMA Awards nominations list.

Gallery Credit: Billy Dukes

Sony Music Nashville

Snubbed: Megan Moroney, 'Cloud 9'

The CMA Awards Album of the Year category is particularly stacked in 2026, so Megan Moroney's miss may not be the slight it feels like. It speaks to a theme however. The five nominees are far more traditional than Cloud 9.

Overall, Moroney only picked up recognition in the Female Vocalist and Song of the Year categories one year after leading all artists with six nominations.

Ethan Miller, Getty Images

Surprise: Kacey Musgraves, "Dry Spell"

It turns out CMA Awards voters are a horny group. "Dry Spell" earned two nominations and Kacey Musgraves finds herself in five categories. The CMAs are not the country awards show prone to letting the freak flag fly so this unabashed love is shocking.

Jason Kempin, Getty Images

Snubbed: Jelly Roll

Jelly Roll's snubbing in 2026 isn't as blatant as it was in 2025. He's firmly between albums and not really touring as a headliner.

He makes this list in the same capacity Luke Bryan, Jason Aldean etc .... did for several years. It's surprising until it's not.

The real test will come when he drops new music. It it's good (or at least popular) will voters choose Jelly Roll again?

Matt Winkelmeyer, Getty Images

Surprise: Taylor Swift Returns!

Taylor Swift is a CMA Awards nominee for the first time since 2017. "I Knew It, I Knew You" (from the new Toy Story movie) is up for Single of the Year and fans believe this signals a return to country music.

The chance of her appearing at the CMAs is only slightly better than Beyoncé's return to that stage, but there's a chance! If this upsets you, get over yourself. Every traditional country singer that takes that stage will get more attention if Swfities tune in too.

David Becker, Getty Images

Snubbed: Stella Lefty, "Boston"

Someone is going to claim "Boston" wasn't a hit when the eligibility period closed on June 30, but that's not true. It was sitting inside the Top 20 on the Billboard Country Airplay chart and it was dominating streaming platforms.

Haters are so desperate to criticize Stella Lefty that they're claiming she ripped the song off from Noah Kahan. "Stick Season" fans are crawling out of the woodwork and suddenly everyone is familiar with the inner-workings of GroupOn (her dad co-founded the company and did quite well when it went public).

The are excuses to support an anti-pop bias.

Jason Kempin, Getty Images

Snubbed: Shaboozey + Jelly Roll's "Amen"

Shaboozey now has three Billboard Country Airplay No. 1 hits. "Amen" (with Jelly Roll) topped the charts in 2026. It should have been nominated in the Musical Event category.

Nicola Gell, Getty Images

Snubbed: Rascal Flatts

Midland replacing Rascal Flatts in the Vocal Group category exemplifies this new trend in country music. Meanwhile Brooks & Dunn — who haven't released an actual new song since President Obama's first term — are looking to win their third straight award in the Duo category.

What's the difference?

Jason Kempin, Getty Images

Snubbed: Morgan Wallen

Morgan Wallen doesn't care about awards, so voters have stopped voting for him. He's also between albums and tour dates are becoming precious.

New music would give fans more to be angry about but it's likely they'll still be angry about his snubbing in 2027.

Joshua Applegate, Getty Images

Snubbed: Tyler Nance, "Keeps Me Sane"

This is the critic's pick for 2026. It's great to see Stephen Wilson Jr. getting so much love from CMA Awards voters. Tyler Nance should too.

Both songs are important and both artists fly under the radar with intention. Don't be surprised if "Keeps Me Sane" tops year-end best songs list for 2026.
