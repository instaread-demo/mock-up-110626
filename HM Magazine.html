Architects release new single and music video, ‘Machine’

Global rock heavyweights Architects return today with their brand new single, “Machine,” out now via Epitaph. The track marks the band’s first new music since the release of their acclaimed 11th studio album, The Sky, the Earth & All Between.

Written by Dan Searle and Sam Carter and produced by Searle, Architects push their sound further in “Machine,” pairing soaring melodies, intensity and an explosive chorus that has established them as one of the leading bands on the scene. The accompanying video, directed by Jensen Noen, explores perception and control, moving from a grounded reality into an increasingly abstract and distorted world.

“Machine” arrives following an especially strong run for Architects. Their most recent album, The Sky, the Earth & All Between, ushered in a major new chapter for the band, delivering back-to-back #1 singles at Mainstream Rock Radio in the US with “Everything Ends” and “Broken Mirror.” Architects also earned an iHeartRadio Music Award nomination, further cementing their growing presence.

From their early days playing pub shows in Brighton to performing on stadium stages with Metallica and Linkin Park plus their own headline arena tours, Architects have continually evolved while staying true to their roots and pushing the boundaries of heavy music on a global level.

Following a run of major live performances this year and last, the band are now set to join Korn for their upcoming UK and European arena dates.
